Nov 26, 2026

Above the trees

there are passive wild griffins out in adventure. offer one raw meat three times to earn a griffin’s trust. once it trusts you, you can ride it again from clear ground. climb to pick up speed, fly through the clouds, and descend to a safe landing. airborne riders are visible to other flyers and hidden from players on the ground. land before you fight. the flight controls work on keyboard, xbox and touch.

look for a rare savanna giraffe browsing in the thorn scrub. it goes about its day and backs off when startled. the roads have wandering travelers too. watch for the little sparkle on a traveler’s bag. there are five new tree forms across the biomes and four new rock formations, from windbent cypress on the coast to frost spruce and frost chips in the snow.

on horseback, there’s more clearance around tree trunks. you can still ride beneath the branches, and calling your horse checks for room before you mount.

Nov 26, 2026

A place to come home to

construction has its own build tab. search for a piece or open one of the categories, and place what’s already in your pack straight from there. drag to lay floors or draw a wall. room mode draws the rectangle with a gap for the door. finish the floor, the walls and the door and the roof goes on automatically. you can pick slate, ember, moss or pale from inside, including in older homes that fit the same rules.

there’s an oak end table, a low coffee table and an entry console. set snacks, books and little keepsakes on them, or light a candle or lantern on top. the brass candle lantern is smaller now. it fits by a bed or on a table and gives the room a soft pool of light.

rest is part of multiplayer adventure. it goes down slowly while you’re playing, and if you’re tired your stamina and arcana come back a little slower. take five seconds with a trail bedroll out of combat to get back on your feet. a ten-second rest in your own roofed bed or a free bunk at the ember inn or cinderwatch waystation fills your rest and gives you a short well rested bonus.

use rest & log out beside one of those beds to skip the wait. come back after at least a minute and you’ll be rested. your friends keep playing while you sleep, and ranked duels use the same recovery rates for both players.

Nov 26, 2026

The etching stall

find merrin etch in cinderwatch and bring a weapon, a gem and a rune. the gem picks the effect and the highest tier it can reach. the rune sets how strong the effect is within that limit. you can see the result before you confirm. the enchantment stays with that weapon when you trade it, share it or put it away, and it has a little moving glow of its own when you hold it.

underground caches can hold an ashmark, rillcut, briarbind, dawncut, starforge or hushwake rune. sell spares to verity fell for gold. enchanted effects are for fighting creatures, and the weapon keeps its usual condition and repairs.

Nov 26, 2026

Food for the road

twenty new foods are on the fire, from toasted seed crackers and orchard hand pies to beef and root stew, chicken pot pie and coastal crab chowder. hold use to eat as much as you need. meals take longer than snacks and heal over time. you can save the rest to finish later, put it on the table or drop it for a friend.

the alchemy counter has ten new recipes. there’s a drink for stamina, one for warmth, one for a quick ward and one for getting out of roots and slows. the three new splash potions leave recovery fields. unfinished bottles keep their portions, and the amount you drink matters for how long the effects last. repeat drinks refresh the same timed effect within its limits. each effect applies at a single strength.

Winter lights

the christmas trees arrive at midnight central on december 1 (06:00 utc). there are five looks, from snow and ribbons to warm lights with moving fireflies. small wrapped presents sit under the branches as decorations. compact trees fit indoors and full-size trees can go outside once the season opens. the separate reward present is still disabled.

the floppy santa hat is legendary and cosmetic, so it can sit over your equipment. the mythic broken halo mask has fitted views from every direction, with its ember tear on one cheek. the mask’s drop location is still to come.

Nov 26, 2026

Less redrawing on the road

the camera can scroll past ground and scenery the game has already prepared. it reuses the rows that are still in view as you move in any direction. when a tile changes, its row and the neighboring details refresh while the rest can stay cached.

shoreline details, grass tufts and tree shadows reuse the pieces still in view. the forest keeps its wind and canopy cutaways on foot. in high detail, editing a shoreline also refreshes the banks across the next chunk.

grass keeps the same pixel-by-pixel sway in the wind. it now reuses its drawing buffers from one frame to the next.

on a griffin, the game prepares the ground in the direction you bank, including along the coast. the canopy stays whole beneath you when you fly over the woods. the friends constellation and title portrait pause their animations when those screens are closed.

ducks reuse the shoreline they’ve already checked and update it as nearby terrain comes in. the game looks up torches and hearths in the area it has already loaded, so the lights are ready as you approach. the combat HUD reuses target and buff calculations within each frame.

medium and low lighting keep the same look with a simpler shader. the browser also gets the forest and water shaders ready during loading, before you reach them in the world.

Roads to new towns

there are 37 new settlements across 31 mainland countries, from little coastal hamlets to larger walled cities, market towns, garden districts and towns built around the water. smaller winding roads branch off the highways and lead to them. the local, regional and world maps all use those same roads.

the new towns have over a thousand buildings you can walk into, with doors, windows, workstations and fireplaces. city ground has more variety too, from worn lanes and stone squares to pale terraces and weathered boardwalks. street torches light the way. zoom in on the world map for town names and the smaller roads. aurel vesta is still the capital.

saved homes, claims and belongings keep their ground. cinderwatch, the capital, the castle and the existing highways are still where you left them. quest markers also draw correctly far from the starting area.

Hearthstones Online

you can claim land on the shared server now, not just in solo. grab your free hearthstone from the Property Clerk at any waystone (or buy one for 60 Gold), hold it, aim at open ground and click. you get one 30 ft × 30 ft plot per world and it’s yours for good, even if you log off or the server restarts.

Tides & Tethers

Hey everyone, this one’s a big one. You can now hear the ocean when you get near the shore, put a lead on your animals and walk them around, and light up your place with 10 new lights you can set down. Horses sound way better on the road, the highway riders finally make noise, and I cleaned up the roads so the old ground doesn’t show through. Go explore and let me know what you think.

by the sea

walk toward the coast and you’ll start hearing the waves. it’s loudest right at the water and fades out about 14 tiles inland.

leads

craft a lead (4 plant fibre and a copper ingot) and clip it on a cow, sheep, pig, chicken, dog, cat, deer, your pet or your own horse. you can walk two at once.

click a timber fence post to tie one up and it’ll graze there, even after you log off. click again to untie. everyone sees the rope.

lights

10 new lights you can build and set down: road lampposts, porch lanterns, wall sconces, candle clusters, braziers, pitch torches, shrine lamps, witchlanterns, festoon lights and crystal lamps. they’re in the home tab.

each one flickers its own way, and at dusk they light up one by one down the street.

horses & roads

hoofbeats on roads and stone are deeper and fuller now. no more tinny clicking.

highway riders finally make noise. you hear them coming and going, and other players’ horses too.

cleaned up the roads. the old ground doesn’t peek out from under the edges anymore, and the random wood planks across the road are gone. bridges only show up where there’s actually water.

inventory

hover an item in your bag or hotbar and its border lights up in its rarity color. the hover card matches too.

fixed some hotbar items not dropping when you drag them onto the ground. walls, torches, chests, seeds and the wild seed slot drop now instead of trying to build or plant. spells and account cosmetics tell you why they can’t be dropped instead of just doing nothing.

stuff on the ground

stuff you drop doesn’t vanish on a timer. relics like the Lantern of the Second Dawn never get cleared. ever.

if one spot piles up past 150 items, or the whole world gets really full, the oldest stuff there gets cleared first. nothing that’s been down less than 30 minutes.

leave seeds on the ground for about 20 minutes and they sprout right where they are if the ground’s right. crop seeds need tilled soil. wild seeds turn into a sapling on grass.

the cold

at 40°f you start shivering. at 32°f you shiver harder. that part is just for looks.

the shiver is real art now, not the temporary wiggle. your character hunches, tucks their arms in and puffs out little clouds of breath. everybody else sees you shivering too. your gear and look stay on.

at 20°f it gets real. you walk about 20% slower and lose 1 health every 30 seconds. slowly. very slowly.

if the cold takes your last heart you don't die. you black out and wake up at the last bed you slept in. never slept in one? you wake at the usual spot. you keep everything.

your hearts frost over while the cold is eating them, so you always know why.

horses don't care about the cold. if you're freezing, ride.

riding keeps you from freezing. on horseback the cold won't take your health or knock you out. (you can't cook from the saddle though. hop off first.)

the gloamwood is home. you'll still shiver and slow down there, but it won't take your health or knock you out.

finish act one and your fairy has a few words for you about what's past the gloamwood. listen to her.

warmth

stand within a few steps of a lit fire or get under a roof and you warm up. the menu clock shows what you actually feel, with a little flame or house next to it when something's keeping you warm.

the temperature and clock card in the menus is centered now. it was bugging me too.

cooking

drag a pot of water (untreated water) from your pack onto a lit fire and it sits on top and boils. drag raw meat, potatoes, pumpkin, peas, grapes or watermelon onto a fire and it cooks.

everything gets a timer ring right on the fire. water takes 20 seconds, food takes 30.

nothing burns. when the timer's done it waits on the fire until you come back and press E.

drop it somewhere with no fire and the game tells you, instead of dumping your dinner in the dirt.

snacks

you can eat crops raw now. strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, watermelon, grapes, dwarf apples, carrots, potatoes, red onions, pumpkins, cabbage and peas. hold Use on your belt, or hit Eat in your bag.

raw heals a little less than cooking it. wheat, hops and seeds are still not food. don't eat the seeds.

iron & steel

iron and steel versions of the axe, pickaxe, hoe and spear, plus forgeable iron and steel swords. look for iron veins deep in the caves (depth 3 and down, copper pick or better), smelt the ore at a furnace, then forge at an iron anvil. the anvil has its own smithing tab now.

tools and weapons wear out now. you get a heads up at 10% and at zero it breaks but you keep it. repair it at the anvil. iron lasts about four times longer than the basic stuff and steel lasts even longer. iron and steel axes drop a tree in fewer swings, and steel gets you a little extra wood and stone. duels never wear your gear.

you can reforge some named blades and staffs into ironforged and steelforged versions too.

building

drag diagonally with a wall and you lay down a whole room at once. four walls and a doorway, no shift needed. the preview shows how many walls it takes, how many you have, and marks blocked tiles in red. short on walls? nothing gets placed. no half rooms.

doors and windows still fit into those walls like any other wall.

trees, saplings and crops can't grow on cinderwatch's town, gates or roads anymore. the tree that moved into the front entrance has been asked to leave.

horses

your horse has its own stamina now, about four times yours. galloping wears it out, walking lets it catch its breath, and your own stamina stays out of it. when it's tired it drops to a walk. nobody gets hurt.

the stamina bar turns saddle brown and says HORSE while you ride.

your horse sounds like a horse now. clip-clop when you walk, a real three-beat gallop when you run. no more sounding like a guy jogging.

sound

new indoor fireplace crackle. warmer than the campfire, and louder the closer you get.

restoration

restoration finally looks like a heal. your hearts fill back up with a mint glow, a little +30 floats up, and everyone nearby sees sparkles rise off you. the blue on your hearts was your armor the whole time. restoration was healing you, now you can see it.

magelight lights the way now instead of blinding you. the light still comes from your fairy, it just doesn't turn you white anymore. no more double flash when you cast it either.

the gods' emporium

the emporium got a full makeover. framed item plates, clear rarity tags, a proper "new stock in" timer, and a real message when a shelf is empty. it's still cosmetics only. nothing in there makes you stronger.

calling cards look like calling cards in the shop now. big, crisp and animated in a gold frame with your name and title on them, plus a preview at the size you'll see in game.

menus

you can drag stuff off your hotbar and let go over the world to drop it on the ground. works from the hotbar in your inventory too. hold shift to drop the whole stack.

back and close buttons all say [Esc] now, everywhere. the profile button that also said "customize" now says "edit calling card". two buttons with one name was one too many.

if you've got an old tab open, it'll ask you to refresh. do it.

the animals

  • give a stray dog raw meat or venison and it'll follow you home. for a cat, bring raw crab. you can have one of each.
  • interact with your pet to tell it to sit or follow. log back in and the cat you told to sit is right where you left it.
  • added cows, chickens, pigs and sheep. they drop raw beef, chicken, pork and mutton. cook it at a fire before you eat it.

around the house

  • put a door or window straight into an existing wall. gates fit into fences the same way. take any of them out with a pickaxe and the original wall or fence comes back.
  • lay a hearth carpet over timber or stone, then put the furniture on it. you can sit on the dining chair, oak stool or cushioned bench. the round table holds six stacks of food and drink. friends can help themselves.
  • the stone fireplace gives the room an amber glow and a quieter crackle indoors. sit by it to rest, cook or boil water. two wild seeds and a potato make a Hearthloaf.

on the road to Cinderveil

  • Tamsin Reed, Orren Vale, Nia Flint and Ivo Ash wander the roads. each has their own face, hair and name over their head. hit one outside a protected area and they'll hit you back.
  • you'll see the occasional rider on the mainland highways. if you spot a trader's stall, stop and see what they're selling.
  • once you're through the gates of Aurel Vesta, follow the bronze lamps to the forum. the capital has temple steps, baths and a city watch.
  • run through Cinderwatch or Aurel Vesta at night and the next stretch of street is already lit when it comes into view. once you leave, your game puts those lights away.

stats for nerds

here's the math if you care. numbers are before armor, buffs, or other modifiers.

  • Arcane Slash: base damage is 70% of a copper sword's 34, so 23.8. base cost is 4 Arcana (or 15 stamina if you're short on Arcana), with a 0.8 second cooldown. reach is 1.2 tiles in a 110° cone.
  • Overdrawn: paying with stamina and dropping to 20% or less starts an 8 second window. hold for 0.4 seconds for one last slash: 71.4 base damage against creatures, capped at 47.6 against players. it kills you, too.
  • Iron Skin: absorbs up to 20 damage for 3 seconds, with an 8 second cooldown. the first hit that can trigger retaliation sends back what you absorbed from that hit, up to 10 damage.
  • Shore crab: 24 health, 48 second respawn, and one raw crab meat every kill. one raw makes one cooked. cooked heals up to 14 vitality.
  • Woodland deer: 32 health, 3 minute respawn, and 1–2 venison every kill. venison heals up to 8 vitality.
  • Local map: max surface width went from 256 to 768 tiles. underground goes up to 384.
