Nov 21, 2026

Open Beta. Also, crabs.

the title screen says open beta now. the game isn't done. that's kind of the point.

The map finally looks like a map

  • redid the world map. it's got coastlines, a compass, and a scale that uses feet and miles.
  • the islands have separate markers. tap one and you get its name. no list taking up half the screen.
  • if a coastal country looks like a little sliver, tap it. you get a bigger view of its actual outline. i didn't move anybody's borders.
  • the local map zooms out three times farther. faraway landmarks turn into dots instead of all trying to yell their names at once.

Crabs and deer

  • the crabs on Sedgecrown actually walk around now. kill one, pick up the raw crab meat, and cook it at a campfire. it heals you.
  • there are deer out on the mainland. they're uncommon, and they run when you get close. if you catch one, it drops venison.

Nov 20, 2026

Cinderwatch, Spell Schools and mobile play

Cinderwatch has walls and roads, spell loadouts are easier to manage, and the browser build now includes touch controls.

16 hours, two APIs

spent 16 fucking hours slamming my head into my desk trying to get two APIs to talk to each other. they had one job. so did i, technically.

checkout doesn’t throw you into an empty tab anymore. the game waits until stripe is ready and then gives you a button. revolutionary shit, i know.

Phone and tablet

  • Play in landscape with touch movement, casting and interaction. Drag in the lower-left to move; the movement stick appears only while you drag.
  • The game fills more of the landscape screen, accounts for camera cutouts, and can prompt you to turn your phone sideways. The sign-in form uses your device’s keyboard.

Magic and combat

  • The redesigned radial spellbook matches the Inventory and Quick Menu. The Spell Schools screen now lets you set up spell loadouts and assign Magelight.
  • The equipped spell has a school-colored cooldown dial. The spell panel shows the cost and whether to click or hold to cast.
  • Space once again triggers bare-handed Arcane Slash, with charge and multiplayer swing feedback. A completed Overdrawn strike resolves on the game simulation tick. The final strike can still kill your character, but the save remains valid so you can reload and respawn.
  • Warding adds Iron Skin, a short protective spell that absorbs damage and can send some of the first hit back to the attacker.
  • Equipped armor now follows the animated character more closely.

Cinderwatch and the world

  • Cinderwatch has new fortifications and staffed gates, with connecting roads leading out of town. Lanterns light the streets and the Ember Inn, and selected homes and hearthside buildings have lit fireplaces.
  • Indoor roof cutaways fade more smoothly and stay in place as nearby tiles load. From inside, you can see outdoors through window glass without revealing the whole exterior.
  • Puddles now draw beneath trees, and usable bookcases highlight so they’re easier to find.
  • This release also includes updates to world regions.

Menus, friends and crafting

  • The Quick Menu opens more responsively and hands control back more reliably when you close it. Text chat is easier to read and can be collapsed.
  • The Friends constellation is back as the Social screen. The client includes a referral flow for a 100 Aether reward to the inviter after an eligible new player signs up.
  • Crafting menus have been reworked. The Mint lets you make and name a small pixel-art keepsake.

The Gods’ Emporium

  • Open the Emporium from your first adventure or the multiplayer HUD. The shop adds item previews and a private bug-report option, and chat no longer covers the shop.
  • For checkout, the game stays in view while the Stripe link loads. A Continue to Stripe prompt opens the hosted checkout when it’s ready.

Audio

also fixed the audio. grass, wood and snow sound different under your feet. doors make noise. rain and thunder chill out when you go inside. loading into the world shouldn’t take half the sound with it anymore.

anyway that’s the update. go open a door or something.

Browser and website

  • The browser caches the game pack for repeat visits. Shaders are prepared earlier, and rendering and movement changes target stalls and abrupt stops while keeping the HUD readable.
  • Seyble has a new home at seyble.com, with Cinderwatch artwork, gameplay screenshots and clearer links to browser play and desktop downloads. The Seyble name is now on the game and desktop launcher too.

Nov 17, 2026

Seyble Alpha Patch Notes: "Everything Goes Quiet Except Your Heartbeat"

Magic

  • New spell: Arcane Slash. It is bare-handed, costs 4 arcana, and costs 15 stamina if you are out of arcana. Drain your stamina low enough and everything goes quiet except your heartbeat. One more strike after that kills you. We are not being poetic.
  • Arcane Bolt is in. It has range. Your hands do not, which is how we got here.
  • Restoration is hold-to-cast now. Keep holding. The mint glow is not a suggestion.
  • The Q wheel and the hotbar now show cooldowns and arcana costs, so you can see the bill before you pay it.

The First Night

  • The new storm opening is live: your first night, the rain, and Lio on the trail. He was already waiting. The weather finally showed up on time.

The Map

  • The map opens in about a twentieth of a second now and pans instantly. It used to make you wait. It has decided to be helpful instead.

Loot

  • Dropping items works again. Use the Drop button, drag the item out, or press Z. The little thuds missed you too.
  • Small popups appear when you pick up or drop items, in case you watched something vanish and briefly doubted yourself.

Getting Around

  • The old menu harp is back. In browsers it starts on your first click, like it was waiting to be asked.
  • Y, middle mouse or the right bumper switches targets. Tab now only opens the quick menu. Tab had too many jobs.
  • Movement got an overhaul. You will feel it in the first few steps and then forget there was ever another way to walk.

The World

  • The game runs smoother. The forest and towns are about 18-19% faster. The trees have not issued a statement.
  • Loading is faster, and it's fixed. If an update breaks something, the game rolls back on its own and pretends the whole thing was a bad dream.
  • When a world fills up, it overflows into a fresh copy with the same weather and the same time. The rain does not start over just because you needed more room.
  • Text is easier to read. The letters were tired of whispering.

Coming Soon (Not Live Yet)

  • More spells, and a new spell wheel. We'll tell you when the stars are ready.

Nov 17, 2026

Seyble Alpha Patch Notes: "Please Don't Look Directly at the Shoreline"

Magic

  • Press Q to open a radial spell wheel for Spark, Frost, Growth and Magelight. Clicking way outside the ring now closes it instead of equipping Frost at a boar's funeral.
  • New spell: Restoration. It heals three hearts with a mint glow and little rising sparkles. It doesn't resurrect anyone, so please stop trying.
  • Casting at full health is free. That's not a bug. That's us respecting your anxiety.
  • Skill Sky moved to Tab, then down, then release. K no longer casts anything. K is unemployed now and taking it hard.

The World

  • Forests got thinned out, going from 1,436 trees to 755 in our test area. The other 681 trees moved to a farm upstate.
  • Shorelines are now smooth, curvy and natural instead of a staircase. Our artists cried. Our water also cried, but in a deeper teal.
  • Lakes have reeds, cattails, bank stones and reflected moonlight now. You may now stare at a pond for 40 minutes, as nature intended.
  • Grass no longer shows square tiles through its own corners. Grass is confident again.

Sneaking

  • Ctrl toggles sneak. You move at half speed, the screen goes dark at the edges, and a giant eye tells you whether you're Hidden or Seen. The eye has no further comment.
  • Enemies now remember where they last saw you for 2.5 seconds instead of forgetting you instantly like a goldfish. They still can't attack through walls, out of politeness.

Loot

  • Boars drop produce, seeds and fibre. They don't drop swords, furniture or cooked meals anymore. We don't know how they were carrying a wardrobe either.
  • Gilded Scamps still drop coins and stolen gear. They are thieves, and that's canon.
  • Furniture now actually stores things. Your bedside table finally has a purpose beyond holding up a candle.

Map

  • The map got a fullscreen glow-up with mist, fog banks, serif labels and a north arrow, for when you're lost but want to feel classy about it.
  • Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, and press C to recenter on your ivory little self.

Accounts

  • Seyble now asks "What should we call you?" Your answer is permanent-ish, lowercase and 3 to 16 characters long. Choose wisely.
  • The first player account, @top, is live and wears a teal DEV tag. Yes, the boss made his own account first. No, you can't have DEV. We checked.

The Gods' Emporium

  • A divine shop where you can try on the Gloamcourt Jester hat, rotate it and admire yourself. Checkout is still disabled, because the gods aren't taking payments yet. They're gods. They're busy.

Known Issues

  • Your friends may notice you sneaking. That's a friendship issue, not a bug.
  • Boars are still upset about the wardrobe nerf.
