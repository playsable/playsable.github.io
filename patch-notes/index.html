Patch notes

Nov 23, 2026

stay near the fire

it's a much colder world out there now. the temperature isn't just a number on the clock anymore.

chat and names

you can talk about your assassin class without losing half the sentence to stars. the chat filter recognizes ordinary words and catches more spelling variations. character names have their own stricter rules, including names reserved for staff.

on horseback

your horse has its own hoofbeats now. listen as you leave the road for the grass, or pick up a gallop. back on foot, your boots take over.

hover over an item in your pack or on your belt to see its details in one place. cast Magelight and the light comes from your fairy, with a softer glow on your character.

the cold

at 40°f you start shivering. at 32°f you shiver harder. that part is just for looks.

the shiver is real art now, not the temporary wiggle. your character hunches, tucks their arms in and puffs out little clouds of breath. everybody else sees you shivering too. your gear and look stay on.

at 20°f it gets real. you walk about 20% slower and lose 1 health every 30 seconds. slowly. very slowly.

if the cold takes your last heart you don't die. you black out and wake up at the last bed you slept in. never slept in one? you wake at the usual spot. you keep everything.

your hearts frost over while the cold is eating them, so you always know why.

horses don't care about the cold. if you're freezing, ride.

riding keeps you from freezing. on horseback the cold won't take your health or knock you out. (you can't cook from the saddle though. hop off first.)

the gloamwood is home. you'll still shiver and slow down there, but it won't take your health or knock you out.

finish act one and your fairy has a few words for you about what's past the gloamwood. listen to her.

warmth

stand within a few steps of a lit fire or get under a roof and you warm up. the menu clock shows what you actually feel, with a little flame or house next to it when something's keeping you warm.

the temperature and clock card in the menus is centered now. it was bugging me too.

cooking

drag a pot of water (untreated water) from your pack onto a lit fire and it sits on top and boils. drag raw meat, potatoes, pumpkin, peas, grapes or watermelon onto a fire and it cooks.

everything gets a timer ring right on the fire. water takes 20 seconds, food takes 30.

nothing burns. when the timer's done it waits on the fire until you come back and press E.

drop it somewhere with no fire and the game tells you, instead of dumping your dinner in the dirt.

snacks

you can eat crops raw now. strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, watermelon, grapes, dwarf apples, carrots, potatoes, red onions, pumpkins, cabbage and peas. hold Use on your belt, or hit Eat in your bag.

raw heals a little less than cooking it. wheat, hops and seeds are still not food. don't eat the seeds.

iron & steel

iron and steel versions of the axe, pickaxe, hoe and spear, plus forgeable iron and steel swords. look for iron veins deep in the caves (depth 3 and down, copper pick or better), smelt the ore at a furnace, then forge at an iron anvil. the anvil has its own smithing tab now.

tools and weapons wear out now. you get a heads up at 10% and at zero it breaks but you keep it. repair it at the anvil. iron lasts about four times longer than the basic stuff and steel lasts even longer. iron and steel axes drop a tree in fewer swings, and steel gets you a little extra wood and stone. duels never wear your gear.

you can reforge some named blades and staffs into ironforged and steelforged versions too.

building

drag diagonally with a wall and you lay down a whole room at once. four walls and a doorway, no shift needed. the preview shows how many walls it takes, how many you have, and marks blocked tiles in red. short on walls? nothing gets placed. no half rooms.

doors and windows still fit into those walls like any other wall.

trees, saplings and crops can't grow on cinderwatch's town, gates or roads anymore. the tree that moved into the front entrance has been asked to leave.

horses

your horse has its own stamina now, about four times yours. galloping wears it out, walking lets it catch its breath, and your own stamina stays out of it. when it's tired it drops to a walk. nobody gets hurt.

the stamina bar turns saddle brown and says HORSE while you ride.

your horse sounds like a horse now. clip-clop when you walk, a real three-beat gallop when you run. no more sounding like a guy jogging.

sound

new rain on the roof. inside you hear the drumming, the shingles and the odd gutter drip, and it blends into the outside rain when you step in or out.

new indoor fireplace crackle. warmer than the campfire, and louder the closer you get.

restoration

restoration finally looks like a heal. your hearts fill back up with a mint glow, a little +30 floats up, and everyone nearby sees sparkles rise off you. the blue on your hearts was your armor the whole time. restoration was healing you, now you can see it.

magelight lights the way now instead of blinding you. the light still comes from your fairy, it just doesn't turn you white anymore. no more double flash when you cast it either.

the gods' emporium

the emporium got a full makeover. framed item plates, clear rarity tags, a proper "new stock in" timer, and a real message when a shelf is empty. it's still cosmetics only. nothing in there makes you stronger.

calling cards look like calling cards in the shop now. big, crisp and animated in a gold frame with your name and title on them, plus a preview at the size you'll see in game.

the ash moth fairy is now the cinder moth. same moth, better name. in customize, a fairy's button tells you if it's on display right now or when it's coming back, and takes you straight to it.

if you own the gloam horse coat or a fairy, it shows as owned in customize and the emporium now.

menus

the inventory lost the item details box on the right. hover anything in your pack or on your hotbar and one glowing card pops up next to your cursor with everything you need. just one. it used to stack a few on top of each other, that's fixed.

you can drag stuff off your hotbar and let go over the world to drop it on the ground. works from the hotbar in your inventory too. hold shift to drop the whole stack.

back and close buttons all say [Esc] now, everywhere. the profile button that also said "customize" now says "edit calling card". two buttons with one name was one too many.

if you've got an old tab open, it'll ask you to refresh. do it.

Nov 23, 2026

ember & ale

new blood in the world. pick goblin, orc, human, or one of four elves (high, wood, dark, blood) when you make a character. it's looks only, so nobody gets a stat edge.

the tap cask is here. throw in 4 water, 4 wheat, 2 hops and 1 yeast, wait 3 minutes, and you've got 8 pours of ale. anyone can grab a mug.

wheat and hops are crops now. wheat's ready in 4 minutes and hops in 7. foragers sometimes drop the seeds.

brand new settings menu with controls, video, audio, gameplay, interface and world tabs. you can rebind every key and controller button, and it remembers them.

your profile got a glow-up. your calling card sits front and center with your stats and your whole card collection.

there's a little clock up top in your inventory and pause menu now. it shows time of day, weather and temperature, and you can tap it to switch between °f and °c.

new seyble logo in the inventory. three new loading screens. cleaner menu tabs and hud.

your horse kicks up dust when it shows up now.

if you've got an old tab open, it'll ask you to refresh. do it.

Nov 23, 2026

a familiar face on the road

the purple diamond over a traveler fades in as they come into view. walk closer and it opens into their name. if someone has a quest for you, the gold waypoint sits at their feet.

highway riders keep their name and appearance as they ride. take another sip from a bottle on your belt and it stays in its slot. the same goes for the rest of your bread.

Nov 22, 2026

The cat wants crab.

i checked what happens when two players go for the same falling apple. one gets the apple. the other one's still standing under a tree.

save a sip for the road

hold Use to eat or drink from your belt. bread takes a second and a half. a turkey dinner takes four. let go and the percentage on your belt shows what’s left. food gives those hearts back over time.

a Panacea of Health holds 250 health. drink half and drop the bottle for a friend. the other 125 goes with it. you can also brew splash potions that leave a patch of healing or mana on the ground.

look for a purple diamond over the people you meet. walk closer to a rider, a guard or a trader and the diamond opens into their name.

64 on one highway

we sent 64 test players down the same stretch of highway. look closely at the middle and you can see where the nameplates ran out of room. everyone made it onto the road. the server still has work to do to keep up with them.

before you head out

tap anywhere on the title screen to start on a phone. turn it upright and the world fills the screen, with the belt along the bottom. on a keyboard, Space still gets you in.

tap your nameplate on the main menu to dress up your calling card. sign in, try a background, frame or title, and save the pieces you want to wear on your next visit to Seyble Online. the list scrolls separately from the card so you can see what you're picking.

on a phone, hold Cast and slide your thumb toward a creature. the little gold ring shows who you've picked. lift your thumb to fire a bolt. you can keep walking with your other thumb.

a little gold for the road

mossback boars, gravecaps, bog stalkers and cave wisps each have a one-in-four chance to leave you 1–3 Gold. it goes straight into your purse.

you can put a whisper away halfway through a sentence. on Xbox, press right on the D-pad and pick Chat to come back to the same words and the same traveler. masks also leave your head and the hair at the back of it in place when you turn around.

two triggers on the road

on Xbox, point the right stick toward a creature and look for the little gold ring. squeeze RT to cast at the marked target. Full aim assist gives you 30 degrees of leeway to either side. Light gives you 15. if you'd like to place every shot yourself, switch aim assist off in Settings.

there's an Xbox controller guide in Settings now. left stick walks, right stick aims. squeeze the right trigger to cast. squeeze the left to see who you're looking at. if two travelers are standing together, point toward the one you want.

click or tap the dot or name over a traveler's head to open their card. send a friend request, whisper, mute, or block them there. a mute quiets them on your end. a block cuts off contact both ways. you can still see each other on the road, and the duel you're already fighting still counts.

out on the road

your helmet gets the last word on your haircut. if a topknot fits under the iron, it stays under the iron. and if something gets you out on the road, you get a few seconds among the clouds with a card telling you who or what sent you there.

swipe up or down through your pack on a phone or tablet. drag a stack from your belt onto the ground to drop one, or drag a chair onto the tile where you want it to stand.

we fixed a multiplayer stutter you could see when two movement updates arrived together. your traveling companion keeps walking between them, and an old update has less say about where your feet stop.

before you head out

the title and buttons moved over to the left. the traveler in the plum scarf has the right side to himself now. it's the same little sprite you start the game with, down to the brass buckle. click the traveler and he'll step into the middle. pick a build, a face, a haircut, and the color of his eyes. save it and your friends will see the same traveler out on the roads. hover for the nameplate, then click it to see your calling card. a tap works too.

what's in the chest?

  • wild chests and dungeon caches open like a bookshelf. the first time anyone looks inside, the covers slide off one square at a time. a bit of wood makes a quiet tap. rarer finds get their own chimes.
  • that Hushwood Fox mask the boars used to drop was the same one hanging in the Emporium. shop pieces stay out of creature drops and chests now. you can still find a Hollow Stag down in a dungeon.
  • hover over a find and its square glows in its rarity color. one tooltip tells you what it is, how many you found and how to take it.
  • you can grab the first thing you find while the next square opens. close the lid and come back later, and whatever you left is still there. a friend looking over your shoulder sees the same search.
  • you start with empty hands. drag anything from your pack onto one of the nine numbered slots to keep it handy. if you prefer to tap, select the item and then its slot.
  • build beside the highway. the road itself stays clear, even when you lay out a whole wall at once.

out in the garden

  • an old field used to make a brand new potato think it'd been growing there for hours. fixed that for all twelve crops. the clock starts when you plant the seed and saving halfway through keeps it at the right stage.
  • every once in a while, if you're near an oak or a ripe dwarf apple tree, an apple drops off a branch and lands by the trunk. two apples and a strawberry make a pair of orchard tarts at the fire. you can use the apples for mulled cider, too.

the animals

  • give a stray dog raw meat or venison and it'll follow you home. for a cat, bring raw crab. you can have one of each.
  • interact with your pet to tell it to sit or follow. log back in and the cat you told to sit is right where you left it.
  • added cows, chickens, pigs and sheep. they drop raw beef, chicken, pork and mutton. cook it at a fire before you eat it.

around the house

  • put a door or window straight into an existing wall. gates fit into fences the same way. take any of them out with a pickaxe and the original wall or fence comes back.
  • lay a hearth carpet over timber or stone, then put the furniture on it. you can sit on the dining chair, oak stool or cushioned bench. the round table holds six stacks of food and drink. friends can help themselves.
  • the stone fireplace gives the room an amber glow and a quieter crackle indoors. sit by it to rest, cook or boil water. two wild seeds and a potato make a Hearthloaf.

on the road to Cinderveil

  • Tamsin Reed, Orren Vale, Nia Flint and Ivo Ash wander the roads. each has their own face, hair and name over their head. hit one outside a protected area and they'll hit you back.
  • you'll see the occasional rider on the mainland highways. if you spot a trader's stall, stop and see what they're selling.
  • once you're through the gates of Aurel Vesta, follow the bronze lamps to the forum. the capital has temple steps, baths and a city watch.
  • run through Cinderwatch or Aurel Vesta at night and the next stretch of street is already lit when it comes into view. once you leave, your game puts those lights away.

the Gods' Emporium

the shop preview shows what you'll actually equip. try the Lantern Wisp and you can see the little purple thing floating by your shoulder before you spend any Aether.

Water

you can actually see your reflection in the water now.

Crabs and deer

  • the crabs on Sedgecrown actually walk around now. kill one, pick up the raw crab meat, and cook it at a campfire. it heals you.
  • there are deer out on the mainland. they're uncommon, and they run when you get close. if you catch one, it drops venison.
stats for nerds

here's the math if you care. numbers are before armor, buffs, or other modifiers.

  • Arcane Slash: base damage is 70% of a copper sword's 34, so 23.8. base cost is 4 Arcana (or 15 stamina if you're short on Arcana), with a 0.8 second cooldown. reach is 1.2 tiles in a 110° cone.
  • Overdrawn: paying with stamina and dropping to 20% or less starts an 8 second window. hold for 0.4 seconds for one last slash: 71.4 base damage against creatures, capped at 47.6 against players. it kills you, too.
  • Iron Skin: absorbs up to 20 damage for 3 seconds, with an 8 second cooldown. the first hit that can trigger retaliation sends back what you absorbed from that hit, up to 10 damage.
  • Shore crab: 24 health, 48 second respawn, and one raw crab meat every kill. one raw makes one cooked. cooked heals up to 14 vitality.
  • Woodland deer: 32 health, 3 minute respawn, and 1–2 venison every kill. venison heals up to 8 vitality.
  • Local map: max surface width went from 256 to 768 tiles. underground goes up to 384.

Nov 20, 2026

Cinderwatch got walls. your phone got controls.

put roads and walls around Cinderwatch, made it easier to set up spells, and got touch controls into the browser build.

16 hours, two APIs

spent 16 fucking hours slamming my head into my desk trying to get two APIs to talk to each other. they had one job. so did i, technically.

checkout doesn’t throw you into an empty tab anymore. the game waits until stripe is ready and then gives you a button. revolutionary shit, i know.

phones and tablets

  • turn your phone sideways to move, cast and interact. drag in the bottom left to move. the stick only shows up while you're dragging.
  • the game uses more of the screen in landscape, leaves room for camera cutouts, and can ask you to turn your phone sideways. sign-in uses your phone or tablet's own keyboard.

spells and combat

  • redid the spellbook wheel so it matches Inventory and the Quick Menu. set up your loadouts and assign Magelight in Spell Schools.
  • your equipped spell gets a cooldown dial in its school's color. the spell panel tells you what it costs and whether to click or hold.
  • Space does bare-handed Arcane Slash again, with charge feedback and swings other players can see. once you finish charging an Overdrawn strike, it goes off on a game tick. that last hit can still kill you. it won't break your save, so you can reload and respawn.
  • added Iron Skin to Warding. it soaks up damage for a bit and can send part of the first hit back at whoever hit you.
  • equipped armor follows your character's animations more closely now.

Cinderwatch and everywhere else

  • Cinderwatch has walls, guards at the gates and roads that actually lead out of town. lanterns light the streets and the Ember Inn. some homes and hearthside buildings have lit fireplaces, too.
  • roofs fade out more smoothly when you go inside and don't jump around as nearby tiles load. you can look out a window without seeing the whole outdoors at once.
  • puddles show up under trees now. bookcases you can use light up so you can actually find them.
  • updated some of the world regions, too.

menus, friends and crafting

  • the Quick Menu opens faster and gives you your controls back when you close it. chat is easier to read, and you can tuck it away.
  • the Friends constellation is back in Social. there's a referral option in the game for the inviter to get 100 Aether after an eligible new player signs up.
  • redid the crafting menus. the Mint lets you draw and name a little pixel-art keepsake.

the Gods’ Emporium

  • you can get to the shop from your first adventure or the multiplayer HUD. added item previews and a private bug report option. chat stays out of the way now.
  • the game stays on screen while Stripe loads. hit Continue to Stripe once the button is ready to open checkout.

Audio

also fixed the audio. grass, wood and snow sound different under your feet. doors make noise. rain and thunder chill out when you go inside. loading into the world shouldn’t take half the sound with it anymore.

anyway that’s the update. go open a door or something.

browser and website

  • the browser keeps the game pack for next time. shaders get set up earlier, and i worked on the hitches and sudden stops without making the HUD harder to read.
  • the game's at seyble.com now. there's Cinderwatch art, screenshots and easier-to-find links to play in-browser or download the game. the game and desktop launcher both say Seyble now.

magic
